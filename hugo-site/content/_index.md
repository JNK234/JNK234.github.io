## Recent Work

**[Customer-Service Agent RL Environment](/projects/customer-service-agent-rl-environment/)** — A τ²-bench-based environment where an LLM customer with a hidden goal talks to a tool-using agent. The reward gates on the database diff before an LLM judge checks consent and honesty. Red-teaming found the judge accepting forged "CUSTOMER: Yes" lines; speaker-tagged messages fixed it.

**[Sycophancy Recovery Study](/projects/sycophancy-recovery-study/)** — I fine-tuned sycophancy into Qwen3-8B and compared five ways to remove it. DPO looked fixed to a 72B judge, but a probe still found the disposition and it relearned in 5 steps. Constitutional-AI preference pairs did best. [Six-part write-up](/posts/).

**[LEAR](/projects/lear-llm-driven-evolution-of-agent-based-rules/)** and **[QD-LEAR](/projects/qd-lear-quality-diversity-in-llm-evolved-agents/)** — LLM-driven evolution of agent-based rules (GECCO '25, ALife 2025), at Northwestern's CCL Lab.
